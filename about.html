Diagnostic Capabilities

In-House Quality & Anti-Counterfeit Laboratory

State-of-the-art diagnostic instruments dedicated to verifying component authenticity, packaging integrity, and electrical reliability before shipment.

EQUIP-01 • RADIOGRAPHY

High-Resolution X-Ray Inspection System

Non-destructive internal structural examination verifying silicon die dimensions, wire bonding integrity, and detecting counterfeit empty packages or broken bond wires.

Resolution: 0.5µm
EQUIP-02 • OPTICAL

Automated Optical Inspection (AOI)

High-speed multi-angle optical scanning for component coplanarity, lead pitch verification, and automated top-surface laser marking OCR recognition.

Speed: 400 parts/min
EQUIP-03 • MICROSCOPY

Metallurgical High-Power Microscope

Magnification up to 1000x for inspecting package surface texture, sanding scratches from remarking, pin oxidation, and lead plating uniformity.

Magnification: 40x - 1000x
EQUIP-04 • DECAPSULATION

Chemical Acid Decapsulation Unit

Precision nitric/sulfuric acid etching to expose the internal silicon die, validating original manufacturer logos, part numbers, and mask revision codes.

DPA Analysis Standard
EQUIP-05 • SOLDERABILITY

Automated Solderability Test Bath

Evaluates component lead wettability and solder coverage according to MIL-STD-202 Method 208 and J-STD-002 to prevent SMT cold soldering.

Standard: MIL-STD-202
EQUIP-06 • CHEMICAL TEST

Heated Chemical Scrape Station

Detects resurfaced blacktopping and counterfeit markings via heated solvent exposure and mechanical scrape tests per IDEA-STD-1010-B.

Anti-Blacktopping Assay
EQUIP-07 • ELECTRICAL

Digital IC Parametric Analyzer

Curve tracing of input protection diodes, logic threshold measurement, leakage current verification, and pin-to-pin impedance profiling.

V/I Curve Analysis
EQUIP-08 • ENVIRONMENTAL

Environmental Stress Chamber

Simulates extreme operating conditions with thermal shock cycling (-50°C to +150°C) and constant relative humidity aging up to 98% RH.

Range: -50°C to +150°C
EQUIP-09 • PACKAGING

Anti-Static Vacuum Sealing Line

ESD-safe carrier tape repacking, humidity indicator card insertion, desiccant pouch sealing, and nitrogen-purged vacuum heat sealing.

J-STD-033 MSL Standard
Quality Control

6-Step End-to-End Quality Inspection Pipeline

A systematic, documented protocol executed for every lot number arriving at our logistics centers before customer release.

01

Documentation & Barcode Match

Manufacturer label verification, 2D DataMatrix scanning, purchase order traceability validation, and lot number factory confirmation.

02

Packaging & ESD Integrity Check

Moisture barrier bag inspection, humidity indicator card (HIC) check, desiccant integrity, and anti-static surface resistivity testing.

03

Optical & Marking Permanency

High-power optical microscopy checking pin coplanarity, mold indentations, and acetone scrape testing to detect blacktopped resurfacing.

04

Non-Destructive X-Ray Inspection

Internal silicon die size verification, wire bond layout mapping, and frame structure comparison against authorized golden samples.

05

Solderability & Curve Tracing

Dip-and-look solderability bath testing per MIL-STD-202, plus digital curve tracing to verify input/output diode threshold voltages.

06

CoC Certification & Dispatch

Issuance of a formal Certificate of Conformance (CoC), tamper-evident vacuum sealing, and dispatch via express international air freight.
