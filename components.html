Quality Assurance

4-Stage Rigorous Laboratory Inspection

Every shipment undergoes mandatory physical, chemical, and radiographic anti-counterfeit testing in our certified testing facilities.

STAGE 01

External Visual Inspection

High-magnification 40x optical examination of pin coplanarity, mold markings, resurfacing scratches, and manufacturer indentation logos.

STAGE 02

Marking Permanency Test

MIL-STD-883 acetone, mineral spirits, and thermal scrape testing to ensure part markings are factory etched and not blacktopped.

STAGE 03

Radiographic X-Ray Analysis

Non-destructive internal X-ray imaging confirming die dimensions, lead-frame architecture, and bonding wire integrity against gold master samples.

STAGE 04

Solderability & Curve Tracing

Wet balance solder testing (MIL-STD-202) for lead wettability and multi-pin digital V/I curve tracing to verify active silicon functionality.
