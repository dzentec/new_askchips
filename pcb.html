• 01005 Chip Placement

3D SPI & AOI Inspection

Inline 3D Solder Paste Inspection (SPI) checks solder height and volume before component placement. 3D Automated Optical Inspection (AOI) catches coplanarity and polarity flaws.

• 100% 3D Solder Paste Volume Check
• High-Speed 3D Optical Verification
• Zero Solder Bridge Tolerance

Transmission 3D X-Ray

Dedicated high-magnification transmission X-ray diagnostic stations inspecting solder voids beneath BGA balls, QFN thermal pads, and hidden lead-less packages.

• <15% BGA Void Ratio Standard
• Micro-Crack & Bridging Detection
• Detailed Radiographic Inspection Logs

Conformal Coating & FCT

Automated selective robotic conformal coating (silicone/acrylic/polyurethane), firmware flashing, and custom bed-of-nails Functional Circuit Testing (FCT).
